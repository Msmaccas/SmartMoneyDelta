# Pricing

SmartMoneyDelta is released under an open‑source licence for evaluation and personal use.  
The following pricing tiers are illustrative and **not yet enforced** – contact us for details.

| Tier | Description | Monthly price* |
|---|---|---|
| **Community** | Free access to the core divergence engine, fixture data, API and web dashboard. No support or live data connectors. | **Free** |
| **Pro** | Adds live SEC filings, basic storage, scheduled runs and email reports. Suitable for individual traders and researchers. | **US$49** |
| **Team** | Everything in Pro plus multi‑user access, on‑prem deployment, database persistence and custom scoring rules. | **US$199** |
| **Enterprise** | Custom integrations with proprietary datasets, dedicated support, on‑boarding and compliance features. | *Contact us* |

\*Prices are indicative and subject to change. Taxes may apply depending on jurisdiction. Academic discounts available on request.

The open‑source version in this repository corresponds to the **Community** tier and is provided for educational and evaluation purposes only.
